## English
**Name:** Car Service Archiver (pending Partner Center reservation)

**Short description:** Organize vehicle, customer and workshop service records locally on your PC.

**Description:** Car Service Archiver is a Windows desktop app for managing workshop customers, vehicles, parts and service histories. Features include service photos, service history, a recycle bin, CSV exports, PDF/printing and user-initiated backups. Records are designed to be stored at a user-selected local location; users are responsible for protecting their backups.
